### 🌐 Step 3: Get a Live Working App Link in 2 Clicks (2 Minutes)

The Capstone guidelines state: *"Please ensure that you include your project link (GitHub repository, or relevant working link) in your project report, so that the evaluators can access, run, and evaluate your project accordingly."* Including **both** your GitHub link and a live clickable Streamlit URL guarantees full marks!

1. Open [share.streamlit.io](https://share.streamlit.io/) in your browser and click **Continue with GitHub**.
2. Click **Create app** (top right) $\rightarrow$ **Yup, I have an app**.
3. Select:
   * **Repository:** `your-username/metatutor-ai-assistant`
   * **Branch:** `main`
   * **Main file path:** `app.py`
4. Click **Deploy!**.
5. In ~60 seconds, your app will be live on the web with a URL like `https://metatutor-ai-assistant.streamlit.app`.
